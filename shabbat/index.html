Shabbat

for E.P.

on Shabbat, I called
my brother David

strangely, though we are
soulmates but unrelated,
I fall into his footsteps:

‘Dave, what the hell
am I supposed to do?’

He says: nothing changes,
the stakes are different but
you show up just the same

‘What do I do then, Dave,
if this goes anywhere?’

He says: everything changes,
you figure out who you
need to be, to be there

‘I cannot provide, I think,
the things she needs, Dave?’

He says: you haven’t changed,
so I know you will, but
it is very hard work

‘I’m afraid, Dave, that
I am just a fantasy again?’

He says: you have changed,
usually so loyal, unguarded,
she is not your fear

‘I need to know when
this, Dave, gets easier?’

He says: it always changes,
you only grow closer in
difficulties you overcome

‘How do I love her, David
enough so that she knows?’

He says: my brother,
your purpose was always
to love, never change

on Shabbat, I called
my brother David

I cried as he listened
and cared,
